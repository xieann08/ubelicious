EXTERNAL LINKS TO REPLACE:

1. MEDIA PAGE
Replace YOUR_GOOGLE_DRIVE_LINK_HERE in media.html
This makes every VIEW MEDIA button open your Google Drive.

2. ORDER PAGE
Replace YOUR_GOOGLE_FORM_LINK_HERE in order.html and menu.html
This makes OPEN ORDER FORM and CLICK THIS TO ORDER open your Google Form.

3. CONTACT US - FACEBOOK
Replace YOUR_FACEBOOK_PAGE_LINK_HERE in contact-us.html
This makes OPEN FB PAGE clickable and also makes SEND US A MESSAGE open your Facebook page.

4. CONTACT US - GOOGLE MAPS
Replace YOUR_GOOGLE_MAPS_LINK_HERE in contact-us.html
This makes VIEW ON GOOGLE MAPS clickable and opens your exact Google Maps location.
