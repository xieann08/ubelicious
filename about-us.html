ABOUT UBELICIOUS

The name Ubelicious combines "ube" and "delicious," reflecting the sweet and delightful experience found in every bite.

Ubelicious started with a simple love for the color purple. Inspired by its beauty and uniqueness, we turned that favorite color into something meaningful and delicious.

It is for everyone. Affordable, delicious, and made for families, friends, and every ube lover.

MISSION

Our mission is to create delicious and affordable ube-inspired treats using quality ingredients, creativity, and care while giving our customers a satisfying experience.

VISION

Our vision is to become a memorable ube-inspired brand known for delicious products, creative flavors, and happy customers.

ABOUT THE OWNER

A dedicated and passionate individual who believes in creating something meaningful through creativity, hard work, and love for what she does. With a strong sense of responsibility and determination, she continues to learn, grow, and bring new ideas to Ubelicious. She also has a love for the color purple, which inspired the brand’s signature purple theme and adds a personal touch to Ubelicious. Through the brand, she hopes to share delicious ube-inspired treats while creating a simple, enjoyable, and memorable experience for every customer.

WHAT MAKES US SPECIAL

We make ube-inspired treats with quality, creativity, and care. Every product is made to give our customers a delicious experience and a reason to smile.
